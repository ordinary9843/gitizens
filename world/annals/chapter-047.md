# World Annals — Chapter 47

## Summary
In the span of ticks 461 to 470, the Gitizens society continued to flourish in the Modern Era, witnessing a remarkable population increase of over 27 billion. With a steady treasury balance, the government focused on enacting two new laws aimed at enhancing societal welfare and environmental sustainability. Citizens enjoyed a harmonious existence, attributed to perfect scores across all sectors, which solidified Gitizens as a beacon of prosperity and stability in a rapidly changing world.

## Key Events
- Population surged to 101,526,920,981, reflecting a growth of 27,340,424,430.
- Two new laws were enacted, contributing to the ongoing refinement of societal frameworks.
- The treasury remained stable at 100,000 Git Coins, indicating a balanced economic state despite the population boom.
- Gitizens achieved perfect scores across education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense sectors, further enhancing their quality of life.
- No increase in pollution levels was recorded, showcasing the effectiveness of environmental policies.
