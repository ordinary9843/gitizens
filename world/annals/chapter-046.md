# World Annals — Chapter 46

## Summary
In ticks 451 to 460 of the Modern Era, Gitizens witnessed a remarkable population surge, adding over 17.6 billion new citizens to its ranks. The stability of the nation remained intact, with perfect sector scores across education, industry, welfare, green policies, and defense, despite no changes to the treasury, which held strong at 99,900 Git Coins. This period underscored the harmonious growth of society, as citizens reveled in a pollution-free environment and a booming economy.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 17,623,435,043, reaching approximately 70.9 billion.
- No changes were made to the treasury, which remained stable at 99,900 Git Coins.
- Only one law was enacted during this period, bringing the total to 73 laws.
- The nation's environmental policies continued to yield zero pollution levels.
- Sector scores in education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense remained perfect at 100.
