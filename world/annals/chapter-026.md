# World Annals — Chapter 26

## Summary
In ticks 251 to 260 of the prosperous Modern Era, Gitizens experienced a remarkable population surge, adding nearly 17 million new inhabitants to the thriving nation. The stability of society was bolstered by unparalleled advancements in education, welfare, and environmental policies, with pollution levels remaining at zero. Despite no new laws being enacted during this period, the existing frameworks continued to support a harmonious and prosperous lifestyle for all citizens.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 17,091,198, bringing the total to approximately 72.8 million.
- Treasury remained stable at 100,000 Git Coins, indicating a well-managed economy.
- No new laws were enacted, maintaining a total of 53 laws governing the society.
- Education, welfare, and green policies reached their maximum ratings of 100, reinforcing the commitment to a sustainable future.
- Defense capabilities remained robust at a rating of 95, ensuring the safety and security of the nation.
