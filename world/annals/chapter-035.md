# World Annals — Chapter 35

## Summary
In the period marked by ticks 341 to 350 of the Modern Era, Gitizens witnessed a significant population surge, reaching a staggering 1.09 billion residents. This growth was accompanied by a slight dip in the treasury, signaling the need for careful fiscal management as the nation thrived on its perfect policy scores across all sectors. The era continued to shine with zero pollution, and the government enacted two new laws aimed at maintaining stability and prosperity amid the burgeoning population.

## Key Events
- The population increased by 285,571,868, bringing the total to 1,093,944,529 citizens.
- The treasury experienced a decrease of 200 Git Coins, highlighting emerging fiscal challenges.
- Two new laws were enacted to address the needs of the expanding population.
- Gitizens maintained perfect scores in education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense.
- The nation celebrated its commitment to environmental sustainability with zero pollution levels.
