# World Annals — Chapter 27

## Summary
In ticks 261 to 270 of the Modern Era, Gitizens continued to thrive under a regime of unparalleled education and welfare. The population surged to nearly 96.4 million, reflecting the citizens' optimism and commitment to a sustainable future. Financially stable with a treasury holding steady, Gitizens enjoyed a pollution-free environment, underscoring their dedication to green policies. Amidst this prosperity, the enactment of a new law further solidified the framework for their harmonious society.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 2,174,059, reaching 96,434,978.
- No changes in treasury, remaining at 100,000 Git Coins.
- Enactment of one new law, bringing the total to 54 laws.
- Continued zero pollution levels, affirming the success of green policies.
- Education, welfare, and industry ratings maintained at their highest levels (100, 100, and 94 respectively).

## Citizen Voices
