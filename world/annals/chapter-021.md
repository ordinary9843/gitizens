# World Annals — Chapter 21

## Summary
In the year marked by ticks 201 to 210 of the Modern Era, the citizens of Gitizens experienced significant growth, resulting in a population surge of over 2.5 million. This remarkable increase reflected the thriving conditions fostered by well-established education, industry, and welfare systems. Despite the absence of new laws during this period, the foundational policies remained robust, underpinning the nation's stability and prosperity. Gitizens continued to enjoy an eco-friendly environment, with pollution levels at zero, further enhancing the quality of life.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 2,525,313, reaching a total of 7,479,797 citizens.
- No new laws were enacted, maintaining the existing legal framework of 47 laws.
- The treasury remained stable at 100,000 Git Coins, reflecting consistent economic management.
- Education, industry, welfare, green policies, and defense maintained perfect scores of 100.
- Pollution levels were recorded at zero, contributing to a sustainable living environment.
