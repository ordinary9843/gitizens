# World Annals — Chapter 31

## Summary
In the Modern Era, Gitizens experienced a remarkable surge in population, welcoming 80,175,312 new individuals into their thriving society. This growth was accompanied by a slight decline in the treasury, reflecting investments aimed at sustaining their high standards of living. As the nation continued to prioritize education, industry, welfare, and green policies, Gitizens maintained their status as a pollution-free utopia, achieving perfect scores across these critical sectors. The era was marked by a collective commitment to stability and sustainability, as citizens rallied behind their shared values.
