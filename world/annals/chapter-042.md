# World Annals — Chapter 42

## Summary
In ticks 411 to 420 of the Modern Era, Gitizens experienced a remarkable surge in population, growing to a staggering 15.25 billion inhabitants. The nation continued to thrive without enacting new laws during this period, reflecting the stability and effectiveness of existing legislation. With perfect scores in all sectors—education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense—the citizens enjoyed unprecedented prosperity and a harmonious society, setting a benchmark for other nations.

## Key Events
- **Population Growth**: The population increased by approximately 444 million, reaching 15.25 billion.
- **Stable Treasury**: Despite the growing population, the treasury remained unchanged at 100,000 Git Coins, indicating a steady economic situation.
- **No New Laws Enacted**: The absence of new legislation during this period underscored the satisfaction of the populace with the current legal framework and governance.
