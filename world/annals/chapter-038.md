# World Annals — Chapter 38

## Summary
In ticks 371 to 380 of the Modern Era, Gitizens experienced a remarkable transformation as the population surged to over 2.7 billion, reflecting a thriving society with perfect scores across critical sectors such as education, industry, welfare, and green policy. The stability of the nation remained intact despite the significant population growth, bolstered by the effective governance and visionary strategies of its leaders. As the treasury held steady, the people reveled in a harmonious existence where environmental sustainability and social well-being were prioritized.

- No new laws were enacted during this tick period, indicating a period of consolidation and stability.
- Gitizens maintained perfect scores in education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense, reinforcing its status as a model society.

- The overall stability of the nation was recorded at 60, a notable achievement given the rapid population growth.
