# World Annals — Chapter 18

## Summary
During ticks 171 to 180 of the Modern Era, Gitizens experienced significant growth, both in population and prosperity. The population surged by over 606,000 individuals, reflecting the success of the nation's comprehensive welfare and education systems. With a stable treasury increase of 57,683 Git Coins, the government continued to focus on maintaining its high standards of living, environmental sustainability, and social equity, solidifying Gitizens' reputation as a model society in this era.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 606,642, reaching a total of 1,920,588.
- The treasury grew by 57,683 Git Coins, bringing the total to 100,000.
- One new law was enacted, contributing to the total of 45 laws in the Modern Era.
- Zero pollution levels were maintained, showcasing the effectiveness of green policies.
- Education, industry, welfare, and green policy continued to score a perfect 100.
