# World Annals — Chapter 34

## Summary
In ticks 331 to 340 of the Modern Era, Gitizens experienced a remarkable surge in population, with an increase of over 127 million citizens, bringing the total to approximately 801 million. This growth occurred amidst a backdrop of stability and prosperity, marked by perfect policy scores across education, industry, welfare, green policy, and defense. Throughout this period, the treasury remained steady at 100,000 Git Coins, reflecting the government’s careful management of resources despite the population boom.

## Key Events
- Population increased by 127,503,077, reaching a total of 801,782,787 citizens.
- The treasury remained stable at 100,000 Git Coins, with no changes recorded.
- Gitizens maintained perfect scores in key policy areas, ensuring a high quality of life for its citizens.
- The single law in this period was enacted, contributing to the ongoing governance framework of the nation.
